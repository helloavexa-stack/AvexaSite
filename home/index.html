The Avexa Story

Growing up, we (JL and Christine) helped our parents run a small food business from our home. Our days started at 4:00 AM and ended at 11:00 PM. Waking up before sunrise to prepare products and serve customers taught us discipline, resilience, and the value of hard work.

In 2023, JL discovered the world of freelancing. After learning the ropes and navigating the challenges, he earned his first significant paycheck in 2024. This proved that freelancing was a sustainable career, giving him the confidence to encourage Christine to join him. Together, we learned side by side.

After joining The Freelance Movement Tribe by John Pagulayan, we learned how to build lasting relationships and develop scalable systems. Inspired by our roots, we launched Avexa.


Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to help skilled Filipinos—especially students, breadwinners, and young professionals—build meaningful careers, while helping businesses around the world grow through exceptional virtual assistance. Every successful partnership is an opportunity to empower Filipino talent and prove that with the right chance, they can achieve extraordinary things.
